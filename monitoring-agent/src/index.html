Consent & Privacy Disclosure

This desktop agent is installed as part of the DeskTrack Employee Presence & Attendance management ecosystem. It uses your device webcam to automatically verify presence and record attendance dynamically.

Please read the following carefully before proceeding:

  • Webcam Feed: The agent captures camera frames and submits them securely to the company's DeskTrack backend for facial verification.
  • Consent: Monitoring and data transmission are strictly opt-in. No monitoring, camera checks, or communication will happen unless you explicitly grant permission.
  • Revocation: You can grant or revoke consent at any time from this Control Panel. Revoking consent immediately terminates all webcam activity.
  • Real-time Transmission: When active, only live video data is transmitted. No screenshots or logs are buffered locally or cached.

By clicking "Grant Permission", you agree to enable monitoring capability and allow webcam presence checks according to company workspace policies.
